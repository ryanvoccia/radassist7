Radiology Translator

Paste or upload a radiology report and receive a clear, patient-friendly translation in seconds.

Drag & drop your report file here
or click to browse — PDF, JPG, PNG (max 10 MB)

It does not provide medical advice, diagnoses, or treatment recommendations.
